# MuSiQue attribution and modifications

Trivedi et al. (2022), MuSiQue. Dataset: https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This experiment builds a global corpus from supplied answerable-development paragraphs, deduplicates exact title/text pairs, assigns content-hash IDs, and selects disjoint balanced local development/test question subsets. These are modifications, not an official full-Wikipedia MuSiQue evaluation. Source hashes, selection rules, exclusions and corpus mappings are archived. Model weights are not redistributed. Basic-failure recovery is posthoc conditional analysis; it is not a population estimate of all hard questions.
