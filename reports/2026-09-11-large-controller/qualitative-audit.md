## What the new retrieval actually contributed

14B adds at least one annotated support on 6/12 questions; 122B does so on 5/12. These are not equivalent to useful completed chains. 14B repeats a prior query on 9/12; 122B on 6/12. Both use fresh, separate greedy final calls after controller decisions. The explicit final UNKNOWN and wrong-entity outputs therefore cannot be attributed to scoring the SEARCH action as the answer.

- **Veoh, 122B:** initial song paragraph names Papa Roach; round 2 supplies “Vacaville, California, formed in 1993”; round 3 supplies Veoh in San Diego; round 4 supplies San Diego as “the third-largest urban area in the state.” All four annotated paragraphs are untruncated. Final “Third” is an ordinal equivalent in this question, although the unchanged scorer reports EM 0/F1 0 against “third-largest.” This is a per-case semantic recovery, not an aggregate regrading.
- **Military, both initial sets:** two non-annotated Confederate States Army paragraphs name Jefferson Davis and his Mexican-American War service with the range 1846–1848. Annotated-ID coverage is 0, but the answer endpoint is present. 122B returns 1848 in both arms; 14B returns UNKNOWN.
- **Dutch/New Zealand:** both iterative paths obtain the complete 13 December 1642 sentence, while retained Kerry and alternative New York/Dutch passages provide the other links. 122B returns only 1642, 14B UNKNOWN. The larger model has retrieved the required date but omits its day/month; this is answer specificity, not a punctuation-only mismatch.
- **Smokey Bear, 122B:** the added annotated Murder in Texas paragraph contains the gold name as a co-star, not as a spouse. Its added Sam Elliott biography likewise omits marriage. Full annotated coverage does not entitle us to call UNKNOWN an evidence-use error on a fully stated spouse relationship.
- **Luther, both models:** the complete Mary paragraph names Wittenberg near its end, but both actual 300-token fragments stop earlier. Neither final context contains Wittenberg or Saxony-Anhalt. Full-source support alone would misdiagnose this failure.

## Alias, ambiguity, and relation checks

Ommatidae→Archostemata is explicitly stated in the retained paragraph; the question does not specify which taxonomic rank. Said Ashour has multiple listed university employers; Cairo University is 551–600, while the same retained texts support 122B’s 701+ for Alexandria University. Keep the supplied aliases unchanged and disclose these two ambiguous targets.

“2012” versus “Sales began worldwide in April 2012” and “1642” versus “13 December 1642” are incomplete dates, not merely alternate dash characters. An additional Unicode compatibility/dash-normalization diagnostic changes **zero** of the 48 saved EM decisions. “Third” versus “third-largest” requires semantic interpretation; it is not fixed by ordinary punctuation normalization. The incorrect Medical College of Georgia output nevertheless receives lexical F1 0.5, illustrating why token overlap alone is not relational correctness.

## Scope

This is an inspected 12-question development screen, with one controller sample per question and model. 14B BF16 versus 122B 5-bit also changes model family, training, architecture, and tokenization; it is not a pure parameter-scaling intervention. It tests text retrieval and final answering, not latent relay, compression, necessary-information overflow, or a persistent corpus cache. Findings should motivate a frozen fresh test rather than serve as a benchmark claim.
